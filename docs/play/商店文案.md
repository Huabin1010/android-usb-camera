## en-US

**标题**

USB Camera

**简短说明**

Preview, shoot, and record from a UVC camera, endoscope, or capture card.

**完整说明**

USB Camera connects a UVC webcam, endoscope camera, or standard USB capture card to an Android phone for preview, photos, video, and audio.

It is a camera utility, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything.

Use it when the system camera cannot open a USB video device. The phone needs USB host / OTG.

What you can do

- Watch: the picture only, and show controls when you tap
- Capture: photo, video, or audio on its own
- Adjust: resolution, frame rate, picture, focus, white balance, and effects

Photos are JPEG, videos are MP4, and standalone audio is MP3. Files are saved on the phone in DCIM/Camera. The app does not upload them.

Also included

- Resolution and frame rate presets that are remembered
- Picture controls, rotate, and flip
- Monochrome, soul, and scale effects
- Phone mic, device mic, or automatic audio
- Video segments of 1, 5, 10, or 30 minutes
- Light theme, dark theme, or follow the system
- Language follows the system, or you can choose one in Settings

The app asks for the camera and microphone so it can preview and record. The first time you plug in a device, Android asks for USB permission.
