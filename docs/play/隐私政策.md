## 中文

USB 摄像头（下称「本应用」）由开发者维护，用来把 UVC 摄像头、内窥镜摄像头或 USB 采集卡接到安卓手机上预览、拍照、录像和录音。本应用不是医疗器械。

本应用没有账号，不含广告，也不含分析或崩溃上报 SDK。开发者不会把你的预览画面、照片、录像、录音或设置上传到自己的服务器。

### 本机上的数据

- 照片、录像和单独录音由你按下快门或开始录制后产生，保存在手机的 `DCIM/Camera`。照片为 JPEG，录像为 MP4，单独录音为 MP3。你可以用系统相册删除这些文件。
- 主题、界面语言、工作台模式和预览预设只保存在本机。卸载本应用后，这些偏好会删除。已经保存到相册的文件不会因卸载而删除。
- 本应用使用相机和麦克风，只为预览和录制。使用 USB 主机权限访问你插入的视频设备。本应用不读取位置、通讯录、短信或已安装应用列表。

### 系统和其他方

Android 系统和 Google Play 可能按它们自己的规则处理设备信息。那不属于本应用收集的数据。本应用不出售个人信息。

### 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

## English

USB Camera (`com.huanghuabin.usbcamera`) connects a UVC camera, endoscope camera, or USB capture card to an Android phone for preview, photos, video, and audio. It is not a medical device.

The app has no account, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not upload your preview, photos, videos, audio, or settings to the developer's servers.

### Data on the device

- Photos, videos, and standalone recordings are created when you press the shutter or start a recording. They are saved on the phone in `DCIM/Camera` as JPEG, MP4, and MP3. You can delete them in the system gallery.
- Theme, language, workspace, and preview presets stay on the device. Uninstalling the app removes those preferences. Files already saved in the gallery remain until you delete them.
- The camera and microphone are used only to preview and record. USB host access is used for the video device you plug in. The app does not read location, contacts, messages, or the list of installed apps.

### The system and others

Android and Google Play may process device information under their own rules. That is not data collected by this app. The developer does not sell personal information.

### Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
