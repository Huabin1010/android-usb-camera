USB 摄像头隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-12。应用包名 com.huanghuabin.usbcamera。本应用不是医疗器械。

USB 摄像头没有账号，不含广告，也不含分析或崩溃上报 SDK。开发者不会把预览画面、照片、录像、录音或设置上传到自己的服务器。

本机上的数据

  • 你拍摄的照片（JPEG）、录像（MP4）和单独录音（MP3）保存在手机的 DCIM/Camera。可在系统相册中删除。
  • 主题、语言、工作台和预览预设只存在本机。卸载应用会删除这些偏好，不会删除已经保存到相册的文件。
  • 相机和麦克风只用于预览和录制。USB 主机权限只用于你插入的视频设备。应用不读取位置、通讯录、短信或已安装应用列表。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

USB Camera Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-12. Package name com.huanghuabin.usbcamera. This app is not a medical device.

USB Camera has no account, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not upload your preview, photos, videos, audio, or settings to the developer's servers.

Data on the device

  • Photos (JPEG), videos (MP4), and standalone audio (MP3) you capture are saved on the phone in DCIM/Camera. Delete them in the system gallery.
  • Theme, language, workspace, and preview presets stay on the device. Uninstalling removes those preferences and does not delete gallery files.
  • The camera and microphone are used only to preview and record. USB host access is used for the video device you plug in. The app does not read location, contacts, messages, or installed apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
